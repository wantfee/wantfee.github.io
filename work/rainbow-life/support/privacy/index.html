Rainbow Life Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026. This policy explains what Rainbow Life stores, how that information is used, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Rainbow Life is a life-timeline app published by an individual developer. You can reach us at wantfee@hotmail.com. We do not run a separate account server for the app.

2. Information the app stores

Information you enter is stored on your device:

People

  • Name
  • Birth date
  • Optional end-of-life date
  • Avatar photo

Experiences

  • Title and note
  • Start date and optional end date
  • Emotion
  • Country and city, if you enter them
  • Photos you attach

Photos you select

When you choose a photo, Rainbow Life reads that photo’s capture date and location so it can fill in the experience. The app does not scan your photo library in the background.

3. How that information is used

  • To draw each person’s life timeline and life stage
  • To show the experiences, dates, and photos you save
  • To schedule birthday and life-progress notifications on your device
  • To sync and back up this data through your personal iCloud account, across Apple devices signed in to that account

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. iCloud sync and backup

Rainbow Life uses your iCloud account to sync and back up the people, experiences, and photos stored by the app. That data stays in your iCloud account and is protected by Apple. You can turn iCloud off for Rainbow Life in iOS Settings. We do not receive a copy of your iCloud contents on a server of our own.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive your subscription status so the app can unlock paid features. We do not receive your payment card number.

6. Notifications

Birthday and life-progress reminders are scheduled locally with Apple’s notification system. They are delivered only if you allow notifications. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

7. Deleting information

Deleting a person, an experience, or an avatar in the app removes that item from the device and from the app’s iCloud data. Deleting the app removes its local copy. Data already stored in your iCloud account follows Apple’s iCloud controls.

8. Children

Rainbow Life is meant for adults. You may add family members, including children, to a timeline on your own device. We do not offer separate accounts for children, and we do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: wantfee@hotmail.com

The terms for using Rainbow Life are on the Terms of Use page.
